# ExpressFind.com — Concept Research & Decision

_Compiled November 2026. Internal document (excluded from the public site)._

## 1. The decision

**Concept:** ExpressFind — *Fast help. Fair prices. Trusted pros.*
An **emergency-first home-services finder** for the US and Canada:

1. **Lead engine** — a 60-second, multi-step "get free quotes" funnel (homeowner leads) plus a "join as a pro" funnel (contractor leads / buyers).
2. **Emergency playbooks** — "first 15 minutes" guides for burst pipes, no heat, gas smell, roof leaks, lockouts, etc. Highest-urgency, highest-CPC search intent in home services.
3. **Honest cost guides** — 13 data-backed guides that auto-adjust for inflation (BLS CPI) every week.
4. **Free tools** — cost estimator, quote checker, repair-or-replace calculator, license lookup, emergency kit, maintenance calendar.

**Why this beats the alternatives for "express find":**

| Option | Traffic potential | RPM / CPC | Lead value | Static-hostable | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Emergency home-services finder + cost guides | Very high ("plumber near me" class queries) | Highest in consumer verticals | $15–$325 per lead | Yes | **Chosen** |
| Package-tracking finder | High | Low ($2–5 RPM) | Weak | Partly (needs carrier APIs) | Rejected |
| Coupon / deal finder | High | Low–mid | Affiliate only | Yes | Saturated |
| Lost & found | Low | Low | None | Yes | Rejected |
| Job finder | High | Mid | Mid | Needs backend | Indeed/LinkedIn moat |

"Express" = speed. Speed is the single strongest buying trigger in home services (burst pipe, no heat, lockout). Planning-first incumbents (Angi, Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor) don't own the *emergency moment* with genuinely useful first-response content; that is the wedge.

## 2. Market numbers (sources linked)

- Home-services search ads average **$6.55 CPC**; roofing $11.13, HVAC $9.49, plumbing $9.39; average cost per lead $66 (CallRail via click-vision.com).
- Emergency plumber + city keywords run **$15–25+ CPC** (WebFX).
- Water-damage restoration keywords hit **$50–$250 CPC** in competitive metros (webtonic.io).
- Locksmith emergency lockout CPC **$25–$45**; "locksmith near me" ~1M US searches/month (webtonic.io).
- Shared-lead marketplace prices: plumbing $15–40, roofing $25–60, kitchen remodel $50–100+ (leadgen-economy.com).
- Pay-per-lead price lists: electricians $55–175, plumbing $60–255, AC $65–325 per lead (Service Direct via clicksgeek.com).
- Google LSA cost per lead Q1 2026: plumbing ~$57, roofing ~$66, HVAC ~$76, damage restoration ~$194 (99calls.com).

### Feature matrix adopted
- **Lead capture:** ZIP-first hero, 5-step funnel with progress bar, urgency selector, lead scoring, UTM capture, draft-save, thank-you page with next steps, exit-intent lead magnet, sticky mobile CTA, pro (buyer) funnel with ROI calculator.
- **Trust:** methodology page, sources on every guide, "how we make money" disclosure, licensing checklist, no fake reviews or inflated stats.
- **Content:** emergency playbooks, cost guides with tables + FAQ schema, video library, seasonal calendar.
- **Monetization:** AdSense Auto ads, lead sales, sponsorship packages, donation pledges, contest sponsorship, careers pipeline.

## 5. Trademark & naming notes
- No live "ExpressFind" brand or registration surfaced in search.
- "EXPRESS" alone is crowded (Express Services Inc. staffing, Express Search Inc. classes 35/45, retail Express, American Express, Express Scripts, FedEx Express).
- Site uses "ExpressFind" only as the descriptive name of the domain; no trademark claim; explicit non-affiliation disclosure at /trademark.html.

## 6. Compliance notes
- AdSense: Auto ads via the provided script, `google-adsense-account` meta tag, ads.txt, privacy policy with Google cookie disclosure, substantial original content, clear navigation, contact page.
- Leads: explicit, unticked consent checkbox; disclosure that requests may be shared with up to 3 providers; STOP opt-out language.
- Contest: skill-based judging, no purchase necessary, Canadian skill-testing question, $100 prize cap (keeps Quebec RACJ filing out of scope).
- EEA/UK traffic: enable Google's certified CMP in AdSense → Privacy & messaging before turning on ads.
