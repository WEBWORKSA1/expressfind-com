Drain Cleaning Cost: Clogged Sinks, Main Lines, Hydro Jetting & Camera Inspection

Drain cleaning costs from simple sink clogs to main sewer lines, when hydro jetting and camera inspections are worth it.

High$600

Range for most drain cleaning visits. Your price depends on your market, the timing of the call and what the pro finds on site.

A clog in one sink is a minor annoyance. Water coming up in the lowest drain of the house when you flush a toilet is a main line problem and needs attention the same day. The price reflects that difference.

Most drain cleaning visits cost roughly $175 to $600, with averages commonly cited between about $230 and $385. Main line clearing typically runs $300 to $800, and hydro jetting a sewer line can exceed $1,000.

What drives the price

  • Location of the clog. Branch drains are quick; main sewer lines need larger machines.
  • Method. Cable snaking is cheaper; hydro jetting cleans the pipe walls more thoroughly.
  • Access. No accessible cleanout means pulling a toilet or roof vent access.
  • Cause. Tree roots and grease buildup often need jetting and follow-up.
  • Timing. After-hours sewer calls carry emergency rates.

Emergency and after-hours pricing

A sewage backup is worth an emergency call: stop using water, keep people and pets away from the affected area and call a plumber. A single slow sink can wait for a scheduled visit.

Safety first. If anything involves fire, smoke, a gas smell, a downed power line or someone who is hurt, call 911 before you call a contractor.

How to save money

  • Try a plunger and a hand snake on single-fixture clogs before calling.
  • Skip chemical drain openers; they can damage pipes and endanger the plumber.
  • Ask for a camera inspection only for repeat main line clogs.
  • Keep grease, wipes and coffee grounds out of drains.
  • Ask whether the cleaning has a guarantee period.

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. Is this a branch drain or the main line?
  2. Will you snake or jet, and why?
  3. Is a camera inspection included or extra?
  4. Is there a guarantee if it clogs again within 30 days?

Red flags

  • Pushing full sewer replacement without camera footage
  • Hydro jetting old, fragile pipes without inspecting first
  • No price before starting

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to unclog a main sewer line?
Main line cleaning typically costs about $300 to $800, with hydro jetting commonly $350 to $1,300 or more.
Is hydro jetting better than snaking?
Jetting cleans grease and scale from pipe walls and cuts roots more thoroughly. Snaking is cheaper and fine for simple clogs.
Why do my drains keep clogging?
Repeat clogs suggest buildup, roots or a sagging pipe in the main line. A camera inspection will show the cause.
Are chemical drain cleaners safe?
They can damage older pipes and are hazardous to handle. A plunger or hand snake is safer.
