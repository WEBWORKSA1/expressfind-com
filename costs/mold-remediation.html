Mold Remediation Cost: Per Square Foot, by Location & When DIY Is OK

Mold remediation costs per square foot and by project size, EPA's DIY threshold and how to compare remediation quotes.

Range for most projects. Your price depends on your market, the timing of the call and what the pro finds on site.

Professional mold remediation typically costs about $1,200 to $3,750, with averages around $2,300. Contractors usually price by affected area, often $10 to $25 per square foot, more when drywall has to come out or work happens in crawl spaces and attics.

Not every mold problem needs a pro. The U.S. EPA says homeowners can usually clean an area of about 10 square feet or less themselves if there is no major water damage. Larger areas, contaminated water or health concerns call for an experienced professional.

What drives the price

  • Affected area. The biggest driver. A problem that doubles in size roughly doubles in cost.
  • Location. Crawl spaces and attics cost more per square foot because of tight access.
  • Materials. Porous materials like drywall, insulation and carpet usually have to be removed and replaced.
  • Moisture source. Remediation without fixing the leak is wasted money; plumbing or roofing repairs are billed separately.
  • Containment and testing. Larger jobs need negative-air containment and sometimes post-remediation verification.

Emergency and after-hours pricing

Mold is rarely an overnight emergency, but the water that causes it is. Fix the moisture source immediately and dry the area within 24 to 48 hours. Then get two or three remediation quotes; there is usually time to compare.

Safety first. If anything involves fire, smoke, a gas smell, a downed power line or someone who is hurt, call 911 before you call a contractor.

How to save money

  • Fix the water source first and dry the area within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Clean small areas (about 10 sq ft or less) yourself with detergent and water, wearing gloves, goggles and an N-95 respirator.
  • Never paint or caulk over mold.
  • Get the remediation and the rebuild quoted separately.
  • Compare per-square-foot rates across bids for the same area.

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. What is the affected square footage and how did you measure it?
  2. How will you contain the work area?
  3. What materials will be removed, and is rebuild included?
  4. Will you identify and document the moisture source?
  5. Do you offer post-remediation verification?

Red flags

  • Selling remediation without addressing the moisture source
  • Using bleach on porous materials as the only treatment
  • The same company testing and remediating with no independent check on large jobs
  • Scare tactics about 'toxic mold' to rush a decision

Frequently asked questions

How much does mold remediation cost per square foot?
Commonly about $10 to $25 per square foot for accessible surfaces, rising to $15 to $30 when drywall has to be removed.
Can I remove mold myself?
The EPA says areas of about 10 square feet or less can usually be cleaned by a homeowner if there is no major water damage. Larger areas call for a professional.
How quickly does mold grow after water damage?
Dry wet materials within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, per EPA guidance.
Does insurance cover mold removal?
Usually only when the mold results from a covered sudden event like a burst pipe, and often with limits.
