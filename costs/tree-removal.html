Emergency Tree Removal Cost: Storm Damage, Fallen Trees & Stump Grinding

Tree removal and emergency storm cleanup costs, what drives the price, insurance basics and safety around downed power lines.

Range for a standard removal. Your price depends on your market, the timing of the call and what the pro finds on site.

A typical scheduled tree removal costs about $385 to $1,800, with national averages around $850 to $900. Emergency storm work is a different market: crews charge a premium for night and weekend response, for working around damaged structures and for the extra risk.

Emergency removal commonly adds 50% to 100% to standard pricing. The cheapest emergency jobs are trees already lying in an open yard; the most expensive involve a tree on a house that needs a crane.

What drives the price

  • Hazard level. Trees on structures, vehicles or near lines need riggers, cranes and more crew.
  • Size and diameter. Bigger trees take longer and produce more debris.
  • Access. Backyards with no truck access cost more.
  • Debris and stump. Haul-away and stump grinding are often priced separately.
  • Storm demand. After a regional storm, prices and wait times rise sharply.

Emergency and after-hours pricing

Never approach a tree touching power lines; stay well back and call your utility or 911. For a tree on the house, call your insurer, photograph everything and get the tree removed by an insured company. A fallen tree blocking only part of a yard can wait for regular rates.

Safety first. If anything involves fire, smoke, a gas smell, a downed power line or someone who is hurt, call 911 before you call a contractor.

How to save money

  • Call your insurer before authorizing removal of a tree that hit a structure.
  • Ask whether debris haul-away and stump grinding are included.
  • Get two quotes if the tree is on the ground and not causing damage.
  • Keep the wood or chips yourself to lower disposal costs.
  • Have hazardous trees assessed by a certified arborist before storm season.

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. Are you insured for tree work, and can I see the certificate?
  2. Is debris removal and stump grinding included?
  3. Will you need a crane, and what does that add?
  4. Do you work with insurance claims?

Red flags

  • No proof of liability insurance
  • Large cash deposits before work starts
  • Crews going door to door after a storm with no local address
  • Climbing near power lines without a utility clearance

Frequently asked questions

How much does emergency tree removal cost?
Emergency storm removal commonly ranges from under $1,000 for simple jobs to $6,000 or more when a tree is on a house or needs a crane.
Does homeowners insurance pay for tree removal?
Policies commonly cover removal when a tree damages a covered structure, often with limits. A tree that falls in the yard without damage may not be covered.
What should I do if a tree hits a power line?
Stay far away, keep others back and call your electric utility or 911. Assume the line is live.
Is stump grinding included?
Usually not. Expect roughly $150 to $500 per stump.
