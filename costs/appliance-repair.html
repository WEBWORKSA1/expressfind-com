Appliance Repair Cost: Refrigerators, Washers, Dryers, Dishwashers & Ovens

Appliance repair prices by appliance, service call fees and the repair-or-replace math for older machines.

Range for most appliance repairs. Your price depends on your market, the timing of the call and what the pro finds on site.

Most appliance repairs cost about $100 to $400, with national averages commonly around $170 to $350. A service call or diagnostic fee of $50 to $150 is standard and is often waived if you go ahead with the repair.

A refrigerator that stops cooling is the most urgent appliance problem because food is at stake. Refrigerator repairs also tend to be the most expensive, especially when the compressor is involved.

What drives the price

  • Appliance and part. Sealed-system and control board repairs cost the most.
  • Brand. Premium and imported brands can have pricier parts.
  • Age. Older models may need special-order parts.
  • Region. High cost-of-living areas run noticeably higher.
  • Warranty. Manufacturer or extended warranties may cover parts and labor.

Emergency and after-hours pricing

Few appliance failures are true emergencies. A refrigerator that stops cooling and a washer leaking onto the floor are the exceptions. Move perishables to a cooler with ice, shut the water valve behind the washer and book the first available appointment rather than paying after-hours rates.

Safety first. If anything involves fire, smoke, a gas smell, a downed power line or someone who is hurt, call 911 before you call a contractor.

How to save money

  • Check the breaker, the plug and any child-lock or reset buttons first.
  • Clean refrigerator coils and dryer vents; many 'failures' are airflow problems.
  • Use the 50% rule: if repair costs more than half the price of a new unit, consider replacing.
  • Check for an active warranty or credit-card extended warranty.
  • Ask whether the service call is credited toward the repair.

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. What is the diagnostic fee, and is it credited if I proceed?
  2. What part failed and what is the total cost?
  3. Is the repair worth it given the appliance's age?
  4. What warranty do you give on parts and labor?

Red flags

  • Recommending replacement without a diagnosis
  • Refusing to quote before starting
  • No warranty on the repair

Frequently asked questions

How much does a refrigerator repair cost?
Most refrigerator repairs cost about $200 to $500. Compressor repairs cost more and often justify replacement on older units.
Is it worth repairing a 10-year-old appliance?
Use the 50% rule: if the repair costs more than half the price of a comparable new unit, replacing usually makes more sense.
What does an appliance service call cost?
Typically $50 to $150, often waived or credited when you go ahead with the repair.
Why is my dryer not heating?
Common causes include a blown thermal fuse, a failed heating element or a clogged vent. Clean the vent first.
