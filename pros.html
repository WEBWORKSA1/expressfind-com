Pro FAQ

How much do leads cost?
Pricing depends on trade, market and urgency. We share current rates with each partner before they accept any requests, and founding partners get introductory pricing.
Are leads exclusive?
Each request is shared with no more than three providers so homeowners can compare. Some categories and regions offer exclusive emergency requests.
Can I choose my service area and hours?
Yes. You set the ZIP or postal codes you serve, the trades you take and whether you accept after-hours emergency requests.
What do you require from partners?
A valid license where your trade requires one, active liability insurance, and a commitment to respond to homeowners quickly and honestly.
