Official rules (summary)

No purchase necessary. Sponsor: ExpressFind.com. Each contest period runs from the first day to the last day of a calendar month (local time of the entrant). One entry per person per month.

Eligibility: legal residents of the 50 United States, DC and Canada, 18 or older or the age of majority in their jurisdiction. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Employees and contractors of the sponsor and their immediate families are not eligible.

Judging: entries are judged by the ExpressFind editorial team on usefulness to homeowners (40%), clarity (30%) and originality (30%). Entries must be the entrant's own original work, must not include unsafe advice, and must not include another person's image without permission.

Prize: one (1) home-improvement retailer gift card with a value of $100 USD per month. No cash alternative except at the sponsor's discretion. Canadian residents must correctly answer a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question to receive a prize.

Winner notification: by email within 10 days after each period ends. If a winner does not respond within 14 days, an alternate may be selected.

Publicity and privacy: by entering, you allow ExpressFind to publish your entry, first name and state or province. Personal information is handled under our Privacy Policy. The sponsor may modify or cancel a contest period if it cannot be run as planned.

Contest FAQ

Do I have to buy anything to enter?
No. No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or win.
Who can enter?
Legal residents of the United States (50 states and DC) and Canada who are 18 or older, or the age of majority where they live. Void where prohibited.
How is the winner chosen?
Entries are judged on usefulness to other homeowners (40%), clarity (30%) and originality (30%). It is a contest of skill, not a random draw.
What if a Canadian resident wins?
Canadian winners must correctly answer a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question before receiving the prize.
