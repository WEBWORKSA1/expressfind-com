Licensing rules differ by trade and place. Some states license general contractors; others license only specific trades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC) or leave it to cities and counties. Direct links go to official agencies; other entries open a search for the official lookup.

Verify these five things

  1. Active licenseFor the right trade, in your state or province, not expired or suspended.
  2. Liability insuranceAsk for a certificate of insurance naming you as certificate holder.
  3. Workers' compensationIf they have employees; otherwise injuries on your property can become your problem.
  4. PermitsWho pulls them, and is the cost in the quote?
  5. Written contractScope, price, schedule, warranty and payment terms.

FAQ

Do all contractors need a license?
It depends on the trade and location. Electricians, plumbers and gas fitters are licensed almost everywhere; general contractor licensing varies widely.
What if a contractor isn't licensed?
Where a license is required, hiring an unlicensed contractor can void warranties, complicate insurance claims and leave you without legal recourse.
