    The rules we apply

    • $5,000 rule: age × repair cost above $5,000 favors replacing.
    • 50% rule: a repair at half the replacement price or more favors replacing.
    • Lifespan: past about 75% of typical life, lean toward replacing.

    Two of three signals = replace. Safety issues (gas, CO, electrical) override everything.

    FAQ

    What is the $5,000 rule?
    Multiply the equipment's age by the repair cost. If the result is over $5,000, replacing is usually the better investment.
    What is the 50% rule?
    If a repair costs half or more of the price of a new unit, replacement usually makes more sense, especially on older equipment.
