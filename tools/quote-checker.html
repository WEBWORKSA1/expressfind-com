Contractor Quote Checker: Is Your Quote Fair?

Enter up to three quotes. We compare them with typical price ranges and tell you exactly what to ask.

A good quote includes

  • Company name, address, license number
  • Itemized labor, parts and fees
  • Permits and disposal, if needed
  • Start date and expected duration
  • Warranty on parts and labor
  • Payment schedule (avoid large upfront cash)

FAQ

Is the lowest quote the best one?
Not always. Very low quotes often exclude parts, permits, disposal or warranty. Compare what is included, not just the total.
How many quotes should I get?
Two or three for non-emergency work. In an emergency, stop the damage first, then compare quotes for the permanent repair.
