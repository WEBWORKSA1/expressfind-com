FAQ

What home maintenance should I do every month?
Check filters, test alarms seasonally, keep gutters and drains clear and service heating and cooling before their busy seasons.
Does the calendar work for Canada?
Yes. Canadian winters arrive earlier in many regions, so do the October and November tasks a few weeks sooner if needed.
