Privacy Policy

How ExpressFind collects, uses and shares information, including advertising cookies and service requests.

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

ExpressFind.com ("ExpressFind", "we") is an independent online publisher of home emergency information, cost guides and tools, and a service-request routing service for homeowners in the United States and Canada.

Information you give us

When you submit a form we collect what you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, ZIP or postal code, a description of your request and your preferences. Forms are delivered to our private inbox through a third-party form processor (FormSubmit). We also record the page you submitted from and campaign tags (such as utm parameters) to understand which pages help people most.

How we use it

  • To route your service request to up to three independent local providers who may contact you about it.
  • To reply to messages, applications, contest entries, sponsorship inquiries and contributions.
  • To send seasonal tips if you opted in (unsubscribe anytime).
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and to improve the site.

Sharing with service providers

If you request quotes, you authorize us to share your request details and contact information with up to three independent service providers. Those providers are not our agents; their use of your information is governed by their own policies. We do not sell your information to data brokers or list resellers.

Advertising and Google cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info (US) and youradchoices.ca (Canada). Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Other third parties

  • YouTube: videos load in privacy-enhanced mode only after you press play; YouTube's policies then apply.
  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google's servers.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

Data stored on your device

Our tools save checklists, form drafts, your theme preference and your cookie choice in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device; clear it any time through your browser settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California, other US states with privacy laws, or Canada under PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or limit the use of your personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising. Send requests through our contact form and choose "Privacy request". We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Retention and security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you submitted them for, and for legal and accounting obligations. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we limit access to submissions to people who need them.

Children

ExpressFind is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable).

Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new "last updated" date.
