Locked Out of Your House: Fastest Safe Ways Back In

Locked out? Check every entry, find a spare, and hire a reputable locksmith without getting scammed.

Get emergency helpJump to the steps

Safety first. If a child or a vulnerable person is locked inside a hot car, call 911 immediately. If someone locked inside a home is in distress or needs urgent medication, call 911.

Right now, in 3 steps

  1. Check every door, window and the garage
  2. Call whoever holds a spare key
  3. Call a reputable locksmith and confirm the total price first

Lockouts are stressful but rarely dangerous. The fastest solution is often a spare key, and the most expensive mistake is breaking a window or hiring a fake locksmith listing that quotes a low price and then drills your lock.

Here is the order that gets most people inside quickly and cheaply.

The first 15 minutes

  1. Check every entryTry other doors, the garage keypad, and ground-floor windows you can open without breaking anything.
  2. Find a spareCall family, roommates, a trusted neighbor, your landlord or building manager.
  3. Check smart accessIf you have a smart lock or keypad, try the app or code.
  4. Call a reputable locksmithAsk for the legal business name, a local address and the total price including trip fee and any after-hours charge. Write it down.
  5. Have ID readyA reputable locksmith will ask for ID or proof that you live there before opening the door.
  6. Insist on non-destructive entry firstMost standard locks can be opened without drilling. Drilling should be a last resort and explained before it happens.

Do

  • Get a written invoice
  • Ask about rekeying if your keys were lost or stolen
  • Hide a spare in a lockbox afterward

Don't

  • Break a window (it usually costs more than a locksmith)
  • Hire a listing with no business name or address
  • Accept a price that triples after arrival
  • Pay cash without an invoice

What to tell the pro when you call

  • Your address and the type of lock (deadbolt, knob, smart lock)
  • Whether keys were lost or stolen
  • Any urgency (child, pet, medication inside)

The next 24–48 hours

  • Rekey if keys were stolen
  • Leave a spare with someone you trust
  • Consider a keypad deadbolt

Frequently asked questions

How much does a locksmith charge to open a house?
Typically about $75 to $200 during business hours, more at night or on weekends.
Can the police or fire department unlock my door?
They generally do not unlock homes for routine lockouts. They will respond if a person is in danger.
How do I avoid locksmith scams?
Confirm a local business name and address, get a total price on the phone and refuse unexplained drilling.
