Cookie Policy

What cookies and local storage ExpressFind and its partners use, and how to control them.

Last updated November 2026

Essential storage

We use your browser's local and session storage to remember your theme, cookie choice, checklist progress, quote-form drafts and campaign source. This information stays on your device.

Advertising cookies

Google and its partners may set cookies to show and measure ads through Google AdSense, including personalized ads where permitted. See our Privacy Policy for opt-out links.

Embedded content

YouTube videos use privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. After that, YouTube may set cookies under its own policy.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect site features. Visitors in regions that require consent will see a consent message managed through Google's certified consent tools.
